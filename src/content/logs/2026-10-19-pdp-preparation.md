## Overview

All group members spent this stretch creating slides and preparing for the PDP (Preliminary Design Presentation). The deck brings together everything since the pivot into one story: why a shared torso limits two-armed robots, what we set out to show, and the design decisions made so far.

#### Deck outline
1. **Introduction:** market trends in manipulation robots, and why two hands matter but one torso fixes the spacing between them
2. **Problem and gap:** the problem statement and the three sub-problems (locate, coordinate, react)
3. **Papers and patents:** CHORUS and the three closest patents (Dexterity, Intel, KFUPM), with where each overlaps and differs from our approach
4. **Criteria and demo tasks:** objectives and pass/fail constraints, plus the co-carry, hold-and-act and cross-gap handover tasks
5. **Designs and decisions:** decision matrices for the mechanical build, software architecture, and hardware (sensing, compute and communication)
6. **Plan and timeline:** a Gantt chart by workstream, targeting a hardware freeze in December and evaluation in February
