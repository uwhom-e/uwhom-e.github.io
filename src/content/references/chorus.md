The closest existing work to our idea: one learned policy runs independently on each mobile robot with no communication between them, on tasks like a book handover and lifting a laundry basket. Its stated limits are our opening — tasks needing exact synchronization are out of reach, and it does not measure what separate bases gain over a shared body.
