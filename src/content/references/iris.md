---
title: "Computing Large Convex Regions of Obstacle-Free Space through Semidefinite Programming"
type: "paper"
author: "Deits, R. and Tedrake, R. (WAFR 2014)"
---

IRIS: grows a large convex obstacle-free region around a seed by alternating separating hyperplanes and a maximum-volume inscribed ellipse. We use it to turn the planned path into a chain of convex regions the MPC can stay inside.
