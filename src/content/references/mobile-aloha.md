title: "Mobile ALOHA: Learning Bimanual Mobile Manipulation with Low-Cost Whole-Body Teleoperation"
type: "paper"
url: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02117"
author: "Fu, Z., Zhao, T. Z. & Finn, C. (CoRL 2024)"
---

The reference point for low-cost two-armed mobile manipulation: two arms on a single mobile base, learning household tasks from demonstrations. It shows useful two-handed behaviour is achievable on cheap hardware, and represents the assumption we want to relax — both arms on one body, driven by one policy.
