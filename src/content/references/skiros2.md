author: "Matthias Mayr, Francesco Rovida and Volker Krueger (IROS 2023)"

Combines task planning and reactive execution with skills described by preconditions, conditions that must hold during execution, and postconditions. This informs our machine-readable skill boundary. SkiROS2 uses an extended behavior-tree model; our orchestration keeps directed acyclic graphs.
