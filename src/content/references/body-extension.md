---
title: "Body Extension by Using Two Mobile Manipulators"
type: "paper"
url: "https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10022877/"
author: "Osaka University & UCL (2023)"
---

One operator teleoperates two independently mobile arms to handle long objects, assemble parts and hand items over — the clearest demonstration that two separate mobile arms can stand in for a two-armed body and reach further than one. It stops at teleoperation, leaving autonomy as the open step.
