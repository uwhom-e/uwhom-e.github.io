---
title: "DART-LLM: Dependency-Aware Multi-Robot Task Decomposition and Execution using Large Language Models"
type: "paper"
url: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09022"
author: "Yongdong Wang et al. (2024; revised 2025)"
---

Uses directed acyclic graphs to represent task dependencies in a language-driven multi-robot pipeline. It motivates our explicit ordering and parallel execution structure. Our typed skill contracts, outgoing conditional edges and bounded continuation search are separate design choices, not a reproduction of the complete DART-LLM system.
